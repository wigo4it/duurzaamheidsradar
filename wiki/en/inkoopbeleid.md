# Procurement Policy

## What does this mean?
A sustainable procurement policy focuses on selecting suppliers that offer environmentally friendly and socially responsible products and services. This includes criteria for energy efficiency, reusability, and minimal environmental impact, as well as ethical considerations such as fair labor practices. The policy encourages the use of local suppliers to reduce transport emissions and establishes that sustainability requirements are included in contracts. Regular evaluation of suppliers on sustainability performance is essential.

- Guidelines for selecting suppliers that offer sustainable products and services.
- Criteria for assessing the environmental friendliness of products, such as energy efficiency and reusability.
- Procedures to evaluate suppliers on their sustainability performance and social responsibility.
- Initiatives to reduce ecological footprint through more efficient procurement processes.

## How are we doing?
In recent years, Wigo4it has taken significant steps in implementing a sustainable procurement policy. This policy focuses on minimizing ecological footprint and promoting corporate social responsibility.

- Selecting suppliers that use environmentally friendly materials and production processes.
- Establishing specific sustainability criteria in contracts, such as minimizing packaging materials and using recyclable materials.
- Promoting local procurement to reduce transport-related emissions.
- Regularly evaluating suppliers on their compliance with sustainability standards and collaborating on improvement initiatives.
