# Sustainability with On-Demand Resources

## Introduction
In today’s digital world, sustainability is an important theme. Companies are looking for ways to reduce their ecological footprint while also saving costs. One strategy that can help is the use of on-demand resources in the cloud. For example, by shutting down test environments in the evenings and on weekends, significant energy and cost savings can be achieved.

## What Are On-Demand Resources?
On-demand resources are cloud-based IT resources such as servers, storage, and networks that can be switched on and off as needed. This gives companies the flexibility to pay only for what they actually use, instead of investing in permanent infrastructure that may not always be fully utilized.

## Benefits of On-Demand Resources

### 1. **Cost Savings**
By using on-demand resources, companies can drastically reduce their IT costs. You don’t have to pay for unused capacity and can turn resources on and off based on actual demand.

### 2. **Flexibility and Scalability**
On-demand resources can be scaled quickly to meet changing needs. This is especially useful for companies that experience seasonal peaks in demand or frequently start new projects.

### 3. **Sustainability**
Turning resources on when they are needed and off when they are not significantly reduces energy consumption. This directly contributes to a lower ecological footprint.

## How On-Demand Resources Contribute to Sustainability

### 1. **Turning Off Test Environments in the Evening and on Weekends**
Test environments are often used during office hours but sometimes remain on at night and on weekends without being actively used. By automatically shutting these environments down outside working hours, a lot of energy can be saved.

#### Implementation:
- **Automation**: Use automation scripts or cloud management tools to shut down test environments at pre-set times.
- **Scheduling**: Create a usage schedule for test environments and ensure they are only switched on during active hours.

### 2. **Use of Energy-Efficient Servers**
Cloud providers are continuously investing in energy-efficient hardware and cooling systems. By using on-demand cloud resources, you indirectly benefit from these improvements.

### 3. **Optimization of Resource Usage**
Through accurate monitoring and analysis of resource usage, you can identify and address inefficiencies. This leads to less resource waste and thus a more sustainable use of IT infrastructure.

## Practical Tips for Sustainable Use of On-Demand Resources

1. **Monitoring and Reporting**: Continuously track resource usage and generate reports to identify patterns and discover optimization opportunities.
2. **Automation**: Automate as many processes as possible to optimize switching resources on and off.
3. **Awareness**: Ensure your team is aware of the benefits of sustainable IT usage and encourage best practices.
4. **Collaborate with Cloud Providers**: Work with your cloud provider to gain access to the latest tools and techniques for sustainable resource management.

## Conclusion
Using on-demand resources not only offers companies cost savings and flexibility but also plays a crucial role in promoting sustainability. By shutting down test environments in the evenings and on weekends, companies can significantly reduce their energy consumption and ecological footprint. By consciously managing resource use and investing in automation and monitoring, companies can optimize their IT infrastructure for a more sustainable future.
