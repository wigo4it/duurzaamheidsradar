# STOMP

## What does this mean?
With the STOMP ordering principle, we give priority to more sustainable forms of mobility (walking, cycling, and public transport) and less priority to less sustainable forms of mobility (such as the car). In this way, we ensure accessible and livable areas.
The STOMP principle is an inverted pyramid, with the top being the most sustainable and the bottom being the least.

- Stappen (Steps): Walking is the very best option. It has been proven that walking at least half an hour a day is extremely beneficial for your brain.
- Trappen (Pedals): Take the bike and get moving.
- Openbaar vervoer (Public transport)
- Mobility as a Service (MaaS): Shared car, public transport bike, electric shared scooter, etc.
- Private car

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it mainly encourages public transport but certainly also walking and cycling. At Wigo4it, you can use your IKB (individual choice budget) to purchase a (electric) bike in a tax-efficient way.
Colleagues who have chosen not to receive a public transport card from Wigo4it receive a mobility allowance. Whether you cycle, walk, or drive and don’t have a Wigo4it public transport card, you receive €0.21 per kilometer.
For commuting reimbursement, the trip is capped at €0.21 per kilometer (regardless of mode of transport). For business travel, this is limited.

Possible improvements:
- Add the public transport bike (OV-fiets) to the handbook.
- Pay a higher per-kilometer rate for colleagues who commute by bike.
