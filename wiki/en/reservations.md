# Reservations

## What does this mean?
Reservations in the cloud are sustainable because they help optimize the energy consumption and footprint of data centers. Of course, this is mainly cost-driven, but it often also has a positive effect on using resources as sustainably as possible:

- When you reserve cloud resources for a longer term, you give cloud providers the ability to plan and utilize their infrastructure more efficiently. This reduces resource waste and increases operational efficiency.

- Without reservations, cloud resources are often allocated on-demand, which can lead to the use of excess capacity that is not optimally utilized. This can waste energy, as servers often remain running idle.

- With reservations, data centers can achieve higher utilization rates of their infrastructure. This means servers are used more efficiently, requiring fewer physical machines to perform the same amount of work.

- Because reservations are often linked to financial benefits, they encourage companies to think consciously about their long-term use of cloud resources.

The advantage of reservations is that you can often exchange them for other types of resources. The drawback is that you are then locked into another 3-year reservation. In this way, cloud providers ensure that you continue consuming resources, although it is convenient that you can always allocate them where they are actually needed. So you don’t throw away unnecessary money by reserving for three years.

If we are completely honest, you shouldn’t want reservations in principle, because they reflect old-fashioned patterns. It’s better to truly only use what you need. In short, use reservations as long as you still have “old stuff” running in the cloud, but think of them like a mortgage. And just like a mortgage, you want to pay it off as quickly as possible ;-).

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it has taken out the following reservations:

- Azure VMWare Solution for three years (saves 52% in costs)

- Azure VMWare Solution for one year (saves 37% in costs)

- Several VM series including E4DS, D4DS and B4S, for our AKS cluster, ScaleSet Agents, and some standalone VMs (saves 40% in costs)

- We aim to pay off our “mortgages” as quickly as possible (within two years)
