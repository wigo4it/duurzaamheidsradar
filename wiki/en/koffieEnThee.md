# Coffee and Tea

## What does this mean?

Dutch people drink an average of 2 to 4 cups of coffee per day. According to BBC calculations, the ecological footprint of a cup of coffee is about ten times larger than that of a cup of tea. If you drink a cup of coffee every day for a year, the emissions are equivalent to driving 640 kilometers with an average gasoline car.

The average coffee from brands like Douwe Egberts or tea from Pickwick is harvested and processed by millions of workers under poor living conditions and low wages. Unfair trade practices mean that farmers don't always get a fair price for their products. Additionally, production has a significant environmental impact, including deforestation, high water consumption, and pollution from pesticides.

By choosing products with the following certifications, you contribute to better working conditions and more environmentally friendly practices:

- Rainforest Alliance
- Fairtrade
- UTZ Certified

## How are we doing?
We have researched our coffee and tea. The coffee is from the Capriole brand and only has the Fairtrade certification. The tea is from Rioba and also has the Fairtrade certification. We cannot easily change the coffee since it's part of our coffee machine contract. This is a good example of how we didn't consider sustainability when originally choosing a supplier. We strive to purchase more sustainable coffee in the future that has the Rainforest Alliance certification and is roasted in the Netherlands under better environmental conditions, such as [Hesselink Coffee](https://hesselinkkoffie.nl/over-ons/duurzaamheid/). Additionally, we could look into short supply chain alternatives like [Wilderland](https://wilder-land.com/).

In summary, we aim to make more sustainable choices in our coffee and tea procurement to reduce environmental impact and support fair trade practices.
