# Public Cloud

## What does this mean?
Using public cloud services instead of on-premises infrastructure can significantly reduce environmental impact through shared resources, economies of scale, and optimized data center operations. Public cloud providers typically operate more energy-efficient facilities and have better resource utilization.

*Examples:*
- Shared Infrastructure: Multiple customers share the same physical resources, leading to better utilization and lower per-customer energy consumption.

- Advanced Cooling: Public cloud data centers use advanced cooling technologies and are often located in regions with cooler climates.

- Renewable Energy: Major cloud providers are investing heavily in renewable energy sources to power their data centers.

- Efficient Hardware: Cloud providers use the latest, most energy-efficient hardware and replace it on optimal cycles.

## How are we doing?
We are actively migrating our infrastructure to public cloud services and leveraging cloud-native technologies to improve our environmental footprint.

*Initiatives:*
- Cloud Migration: Moving workloads from on-premises infrastructure to public cloud platforms.
- Cloud-Native Architecture: Designing applications to take advantage of cloud efficiencies and auto-scaling capabilities.
- Provider Selection: Choosing cloud providers with strong sustainability commitments and renewable energy usage.
- Resource Optimization: Using cloud monitoring tools to optimize resource usage and reduce waste.
