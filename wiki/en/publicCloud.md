# Public Cloud

## What does this mean?
The public cloud is a sustainable choice due to its efficiency and more environmentally friendly infrastructure. Large cloud providers optimize their data centers to minimize energy consumption and waste, contributing to a lower ecological footprint. Here are three key reasons why the public cloud contributes to sustainability—provided it is properly configured:

- Public cloud providers continually invest in modernizing and improving the energy efficiency of their data centers. They use innovative technologies such as advanced cooling systems (for example, water cooling or free-air cooling), more efficient hardware, and dynamic power management.

- Because public cloud providers manage their data centers more efficiently and make longer use of hardware, they also contribute to the reduction of electronic waste (e-waste).

- In the public cloud, resources such as storage and computing power can be dynamically scaled up or down based on actual demand and shared usage. This means companies no longer need to purchase hardware that always runs at full capacity—even when it isn’t necessary.

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it runs 100% in the public cloud and therefore benefits from all of the advantages mentioned above. However, there are a few caveats:

- If you don’t make proper use of the benefits of the public cloud, it can actually be less sustainable. If you simply lift-and-shift everything, you might as well keep running on-premises.

- Large cloud providers sometimes claim to operate entirely on green energy. That simply isn’t possible in the Netherlands. For now, there is always some reliance on grey energy during the day. In addition, large hyperscalers contribute to increased grid congestion—the green energy they consume could otherwise power thousands of households.

- Public cloud combined with 100% automation enables us to deliver more value to our customers for half the cost. Who wouldn’t want that! :)
