# Cleaning

## What does this mean?
Sustainable cleaning in office buildings is an important topic that is receiving increasing attention. It’s not just about keeping the workplace clean, but also about reducing the ecological footprint and promoting a healthy and productive working environment. In a time when environmental awareness and sustainability are central, it is essential that companies review and adjust their cleaning practices to meet these standards.

Sustainable cleaning methods contribute to the preservation of natural resources, reduce the emission of harmful substances, and promote the well-being of both cleaning staff and office employees. By choosing environmentally friendly cleaning products, efficient methods, and sustainable materials, companies can make a positive impact on the environment while also saving costs.

## How are we doing?
First, the cleaner. These days they come once a day instead of twice, at the edges of the day. This not only saves us money but is also much better for the cleaner. It gives them social contact, they are among people, they are thanked, they only need one commute, and they get to have a normal daily rhythm. When it comes to the cleaning itself, we are doing quite well too:

- We use biodegradable cleaning products and no harmful chemicals.
- We sort our waste properly, and it is disposed of and processed accordingly (plastic, paper, general, and organic).
- When it comes to toilet paper, there is still room to make a much more sustainable choice.
