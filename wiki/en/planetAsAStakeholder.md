# Planet as a Stakeholder

## What does this mean?
It’s actually very simple. In everything you decide, build, or purchase, you take the planet into consideration. What if the planet were a stakeholder at the table? Integrating the planet as a stakeholder means that companies acknowledge their responsibility for the impact of their activities on the environment. This has several benefits:

- Environmental and Climate Protection: By seeing the planet as a stakeholder, companies can reduce their environmental impact, contributing to the fight against climate change and the preservation of natural resources.

- Cost Efficiency and Innovation: Sustainable practices can lead to cost savings through more efficient use of resources and energy, while also driving innovation through the development of new, green technologies.

- Improved Reputation and Compliance: Companies that embrace sustainability improve their reputation with consumers and investors, and are better positioned to meet increasingly strict environmental rules and regulations.

There is much more within your own circle of influence if you consciously start reflecting on this. No more excuses for doing nothing—everyone can make their work more sustainable!

## How are we doing?
We’re already doing reasonably well: more and more colleagues in our organization are taking the planet into account when making decisions or building solutions. Some positive examples and areas where we still have room for improvement:

- We don’t yet all practice sustainability by design when building software. Often, we still choose the option that delivers the most performance gains, which in practice means making less sustainable choices.

- Canned water is an example where, from a procurement perspective, we are clearly making the wrong choice if you were to ask the planet.

- In terms of infrastructure, we truly only build and run what we actually need.

- The planet is also implicitly embedded within our organizational goals.
