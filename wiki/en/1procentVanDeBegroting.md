# 1% of the Budget

## What does this mean?
At our office, we believe that sustainable solutions can save the world, even if they sometimes cost a bit more than standard options. That’s why we want to reserve 1% of our total budget for sustainability. This may sound like a small percentage, but it’s a big gesture toward a greener future. That 1% serves as our financial buffer, allowing us to make environmentally friendly choices without compromising on quality or effectiveness.

*Examples:*
- Hosting: The Azure region in Sweden runs on 100% carbon-free energy. Sounds cool, right? It means we can run CPU-intensive batches there, which is much kinder to the planet. Yes, it costs a bit more than in the Amsterdam region, but it’s like investing in a better future.

Sustainable Procurement: We prefer to choose products and services that are green, even if they cost a little extra. We give preference to suppliers with sustainability certifications such as Fair Trade or Rainforest Alliance. That way, we can enjoy our coffee with peace of mind.

User Equipment: Broken phones and laptops? In the past you might have thrown them away, but we repair them—even if they’re considered “written off” economically. This way, we extend the lifespan of our equipment and contribute to more sustainable business operations.

## How are we doing?
At the moment, there is no specific reservation in the budget for sustainability initiatives, but we are aware of the importance of sustainability and the impact it can have on both our business results and our social responsibility.

*Initiatives:*
- Inspiring Management: We need to inspire and motivate management to embrace the 1% rule for sustainability by emphasizing the long-term benefits for both the company and society, and showing them how their leadership can contribute to a greener future.

- On Monday, July 29, the management team gave approval to the memo to reserve 1% of the budget from 2025 for sustainability!

So, if you think 1% is small, remember that this is the beginning of something big. Every step we take toward sustainability, no matter how small, brings us closer to a better world. And that’s something to get excited about, isn’t it?
