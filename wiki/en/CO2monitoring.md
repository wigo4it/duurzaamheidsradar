# CO2 Monitoring

## What does this mean?
CO2 monitoring is a crucial process for companies that want to reduce their ecological footprint and operate more sustainably. By measuring the energy consumption of IT infrastructures and linking it to CO2 emissions, organizations can make conscious decisions about their infrastructure, optimize energy use, and contribute to lower CO2 emissions. This not only helps companies comply with regulations and sustainability goals but also strengthens their reputation as responsible, environmentally conscious businesses.

- By monitoring CO2 emissions, organizations gain insight into how their IT activities contribute to their total ecological footprint. This enables them to make informed decisions about the energy consumption of their IT infrastructure and find ways to reduce their impact.

- Many countries and regions are implementing increasingly strict regulations regarding CO2 emissions and sustainability. CO2 monitoring helps companies comply with these requirements by accurately tracking and reporting their emissions. See, for example, the legislation around ['CSRD'](wiki.html?page=csrdRapportage).

- Companies that monitor and actively reduce their CO2 emissions demonstrate that they are taking responsibility for their environmental impact. This can give them a competitive advantage, especially as consumers and partners place increasing value on environmentally friendly business practices.

## How are we doing?
To be completely honest, it’s quite difficult to find a good tool that really provides valuable insights. You can see that the major cloud providers are working on it, but they are still far from being there. At times, they even seem to be slowing things down, partly because—due to AI—the outlook is less positive than what they communicated a few years ago. That said, Wigo4it is already doing something with CO2 monitoring:

- We use the Azure Carbon Optimization tool, see above.

- We are honestly doing far too little with it and hope to soon start working with the open-source tool - <a href="https://www.cloudcarbonfootprint.org/">Cloud Carbon Footprint</a>.

- We have submitted questions to Microsoft asking them to provide us with more insight into the calculations behind how the data is generated.
