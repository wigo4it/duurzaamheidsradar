# Wegwijzer

## What does this mean?
In the past, Wigo4it used to run more than 80 environments. Environments were not allowed to be deleted—just in case they might be needed again a few months later. On top of that, environments ran 24/7, 365 days a year, while in reality they were only used about 20 days per year. This was understandable at the time, because due to slow processes and bureaucracy, it sometimes took Wigo4it three months to set up a new environment. Naturally, the municipalities were reluctant to let go of environments. There was little awareness of costs, because those costs had already been made anyway. “It’s already running,” “we’ve already bought it”—those are the kinds of phrases we don’t want to hear anymore.

To help our customers (the municipalities) become more environmentally and cost conscious, we designed the Wegwijzer. The Wegwijzer is a portal created for municipalities to easily navigate between different environments. They can also spin up or remove new environments on-demand themselves. It also provides users with insights into the usage and costs of different environments and supports sustainable practices by automating the shutdown of environments outside working hours and providing insights for making conscious decisions. Thanks to the Wegwijzer:

- We now only run 20 environments instead of 80.

- Municipalities are in control of scaling environments up and down.

- Thanks to the credits model, there is an incentive to really use them consciously.

## The Credits Model
The credits model is a system designed to achieve savings by providing market-based and flexible services. It enables municipalities to decide for themselves how many extra environments they need and for how long, based on credits. These credits are part of the base budget and are converted into a euro value. For example, one credit equals three euros, and the total value of credits is €150,000 per municipality per year. Users can use credits for various services:

- One credit per hour for running the environment.

- One credit per hour for support.

- One credit per hour for a production-like environment in terms of data.

This ensures that municipalities really consider their actual needs. Do you really need production data? Do you really need support? They themselves are in control of how to save here.

## Looking Ahead
We’re doing quite well already with the Wegwijzer, and the main goal has been achieved. However, how cool would it be if we could buy live carbon credits on the market via an API to compensate for the emissions of environments used by our customers? A great topic for a future Funovation Day!

In addition, we could nudge the municipalities a bit more, for example by showing a pop-up screen on Fridays with the message:
“Hey civil servant, it’s already past 5:00 p.m., maybe it’s time to log out. And don’t forget to remove your environments—this saves the equivalent of 3 trees worth of emissions!”
