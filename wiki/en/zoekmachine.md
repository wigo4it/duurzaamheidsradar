# Search Engine

## What does this mean?
The use of search engines is an integral part of the daily lives of many people. On average, a person performs multiple searches per day, with Google being the most widely used search engine. The frequency of use is influenced by context—such as work, study, and daily needs—and has increased due to the availability of search functions on mobile devices. Worldwide, around 50,000 searches are carried out every second.

- Environmental Impact
Running search engines requires significant amounts of energy, mainly due to the need to operate massive data centers. These data centers contain thousands of servers that are constantly running and need to be cooled, which results in a substantial ecological footprint. Different search engines are taking steps to reduce their impact, such as using renewable energy sources and investing in CO₂ offset projects.

- Privacy
Privacy is a major concern with search engines, as many collect user data to provide personalized search results and ads. Search engines such as DuckDuckGo and Ecosia distinguish themselves by focusing on privacy protection, not storing or selling personal data to third parties.

## How are we doing?
At Wigo4it, we have enforced Google as our search engine. Employees cannot change this themselves. This is an area where we could improve significantly, for example by choosing Ecosia. The benefits of Ecosia:

- CO₂ neutral, with 80% of profits invested in planting trees.

- Take privacy seriously and do not store personal data.

- No misleading advertisements as the top results.
