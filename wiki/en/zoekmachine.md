# Search Engine

## What does this mean?
The use of search engines is an integral part of daily life for many people. On average, a person performs multiple searches per day, with Google being the most commonly used search engine. The frequency of use is influenced by context, such as work, study, and daily needs, and has increased due to the availability of search functions on mobile devices. About 50,000 search queries are performed worldwide every second.

- Environmental Impact
Running search engines requires significant amounts of energy, primarily due to the need to operate massive data centers. These data centers contain thousands of servers that are constantly running and need to be cooled, leading to a considerable ecological footprint. Various search engines are taking measures to reduce their impact, such as using renewable energy sources and investing in CO2 compensation projects.

- Privacy
Privacy is an important concern with search engines, as many search engines collect user data to provide personalized search results and advertisements. Search engines like DuckDuckGo and Ecosia distinguish themselves through their focus on privacy protection, by not storing or selling personal data to third parties.

## How are we doing?
We have enforced that Google is our search engine through policy. As an employee, you cannot change this yourself. We can significantly improve here by choosing Ecosia, for example. The benefits of Ecosia:

- CO2 neutral, 80% of profits are invested in planting trees.

- Take privacy seriously and do not store personal data.

- Have no misleading advertisements as first results.
