# Message Box

## What does this mean?
The Government Message Box is a digital mailbox where citizens and businesses can securely and clearly receive important messages from government agencies. These messages can be about matters such as taxes, benefits, healthcare, pensions, and traffic fines. Benefits of the Message Box:

- Instead of receiving paper mail, users can access their official correspondence online through the Message Box.

- The Message Box is accessible via MijnOverheid, the personal portal of the Dutch government, and contributes to safer and more efficient communication between government and citizens.

- Its use is free and more and more government agencies are using it. All communication with the government in one place.

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it indirectly generates 5 million letters per year. These letters are printed and sent. Both cause CO2 emissions. By also offering our letters through the government's Message Box, we expect to have to print and send less each year. Apart from CO2 emissions from printing and sending, this is also a cost saving of several million euros per year.

Unfortunately, this is still in its infancy and is for now a thought process that is on the roadmap. This still needs to be further developed, but we strive to have this arranged before 2028. That sounds like sluggish and slow government again, but believe us, that is genuinely fast otherwise we would have done it long ago ;-).
