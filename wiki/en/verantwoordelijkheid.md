# Responsibility

## What does this mean?
Behavioral change in the field of sustainability requires that people not only have the freedom to make conscious choices but also take responsibility for the impact of those choices. When individuals have the freedom to choose sustainable options—paired with accountability for their actions—a direct link is created between their behavior and its consequences. This fosters an environment in which one can no longer hide behind rules, systems, or others; the responsibility lies with themselves.

Without this personal responsibility, sustainable choices remain superficial or postponed, as there is always something or someone else to blame. With freedom and responsibility, it becomes clear that everyone’s contribution matters, and that change should not only come from systems or leaders but also from individuals themselves. This way, people feel directly connected to the outcomes of their choices and are motivated to take truly sustainable actions, because they know their behavior really makes a difference.

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it has embraced the aligned autonomy concept by Henrik Kniberg. When applied properly, this ensures that teams can work autonomously and make their own decisions. The big advantage is that we can release software much faster. Colleagues no longer have to wait for others—except their teammates. Because they make their own decisions, they cannot help but also take responsibility for those decisions. How do we do this?

- Type 1 and Type 2 decisions: 80% are Type 2 decisions and can be made by the team itself without approval from MT, Security, or anyone else.

- No more ITIL, project managers, or architects to hide behind.

- You build it, you run it, you break it, you fix it, you love it!
