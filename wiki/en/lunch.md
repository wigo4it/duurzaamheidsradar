## What does this mean?
The impact of lunches in the business world is significant, both in terms of food waste and the environmental impact of the different types of food offered. In corporate restaurants and canteens, a large portion of the food supply is wasted. Studies show that 10-20% of food is often thrown away unused. This is due to overproduction, poor planning, and varying numbers of diners.

- Meat dishes are traditionally the most common in corporate meals and have by far the highest environmental impact, with significant greenhouse gas emissions, land use, and water consumption.

- Fish occurs less frequently than meat, but is still an important part of corporate meals due to the perception of fish as a healthier option. The environmental impact of fish varies depending on the fishing method and species, with overfishing and unsustainable fishing methods being major concerns.

- The demand for vegetarian and vegan options is growing steadily due to health-conscious employees and increasing environmental awareness. Plant-based diets generally have a lower environmental impact than animal products, with lower greenhouse gas emissions, lower water consumption, and less land use. Vegan diets are usually the most sustainable, with the lowest greenhouse gas emissions, minimal water consumption, and the least land use. Promoting vegetarian and vegan options in corporate meals can therefore significantly reduce environmental impact.

## How are we doing?
Most colleagues get something to eat near the office or bring their own food. We don't have a company canteen. Very occasionally lunches are organized, for example at our sacred quarterly meetings or when clients/suppliers come with a large group during lunchtime. What are we already doing and what are we not doing yet?

- We don't stimulate healthy eating enough yet. There is a lot of unhealthy food available in and around our location.

- We do provide fresh fruit on busy days. Cherry tomatoes, pears, apples, bananas, kiwis, dates, etc.

- When we order lunch, it's currently 50/50. Half is meat and fish, the other half is vegetarian and vegan.

- We throw away virtually nothing. When we order lunch, there are always colleagues who even at 4:30 PM think: "Let me eat this soggy sandwich".
