# Printer-free

## What does this mean?

Printer-free means eliminating or drastically reducing the use of printers and paper documents in favor of digital alternatives.

- Reducing paper consumption saves trees and reduces the environmental impact of paper production

- Eliminating printers reduces energy consumption and the need for ink cartridges and other consumables

- Digital document workflows are often more efficient and reduce physical storage needs

## How are we doing?

Wigo4it is moving towards a printer-free environment as part of our sustainability efforts.

- We have reduced from four printers to just one

- Most documents are now handled digitally through our document management systems

- We encourage digital signatures and electronic document sharing
