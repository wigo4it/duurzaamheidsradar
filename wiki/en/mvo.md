# Corporate Social Responsibility

## What does this mean?
Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) is how an organization takes responsibility for its impact on society, the environment, and the economy, beyond legal obligations. For Wigo4it this is not unfamiliar: we build software that supports people in vulnerable situations. Social impact is already part of our DNA. CSR makes this explicit and broadens it.

CSR is commonly organized around the three pillars of the triple bottom line:

- **People**: How do we treat our employees, customers, and society? Think of fair work, inclusion, health, and the social value of our products for citizens in the social domain.

- **Planet**: What is our ecological impact? This is where the rest of this radar focuses, from ['CO2 Monitoring'](wiki.html?page=CO2monitoring) to ['Green Coding'](wiki.html?page=greenCoding) and ['FinOps Agent'](wiki.html?page=finopsAgent). A CSR plan bundles these efforts into one coherent story.

- **Profit**: Financial health is not an end in itself, but a prerequisite for making sustainable choices over time. A healthy organization can invest in sustainable infrastructure, responsible procurement, and social initiatives.

A CSR plan is more than a report. It is a strategic document that sets direction, defines goals, and enables accountability. It aligns with reporting standards such as ['CSRD'](wiki.html?page=csrdRapportage) and provides a framework for initiatives such as ['1% of Budget'](wiki.html?page=1procentVanDeBegroting) and ['5% of Time'](wiki.html?page=5procentVanDeTijd).

## How are we doing?
Our CSRD page already stated that a CSR plan was planned for 2025. We are now in 2026 and it is becoming concrete.

- Wigo4it is collaborating with sustainability advisors on our CSR report. The report is expected in June 2026, a milestone we are working toward.

- Wigo4it already delivers a sustainability chapter to the external auditor, which is a good basis for a full CSR report.

- This Sustainability Radar is already a CSR instrument in practice: it makes our sustainability efforts transparent and shareable externally.

- A formal CSR plan helps us combine existing initiatives, identify gaps, and set goals we can evaluate periodically.

- The next step is to define measurable KPIs linked to topics on this radar, such as percentage CO2 reduction, share of sustainably procured hardware, and number of employees with sustainability certification.

- Good news: we do not need to reinvent the wheel. MVO Nederland provides strong templates and guidance for organizations of our size.
