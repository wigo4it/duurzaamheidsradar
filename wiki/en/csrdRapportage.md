# CSRD Reporting

## What does this mean?
The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) is new EU legislation recently introduced to improve corporate sustainability and social responsibility through extensive reporting requirements. The key points are:

- Extensive Reporting: Companies must provide detailed information about environmental, social and governance (ESG) factors.

- Standardized Standards: Standardized reporting standards are used to improve transparency and comparability of data.

- External Verification: The information provided must be verified by an external party to ensure reliability.

Depending on the type of company or size in number of personnel and reserves on the balance sheet, these are the main dates when reporting must be done:

- 2024: Large enterprises already covered by the NFRD (Non-Financial Reporting Directive) (more than 500 employees).
- 2025: All large enterprises (more than 250 employees, turnover > 40 million euros or balance sheet total > 20 million euros).
- 2026: Listed small and medium-sized enterprises. In addition, all small and non-complex credit institutions and captive insurance companies.

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it does not fall under any of the above organizations and does not meet the requirements in terms of number of personnel and balance sheet. Therefore, we do not have to report anything for the CSRD for now. That doesn't mean we don't consider it important and we are already taking a number of actions that prepare for CSRD reporting in the future such as:

- Generating transparency through this Sustainability Radar
- Reserving 1% of the budget for sustainability
- Already providing a sustainability chapter to the auditor
- Having a CSR planned for 2025
