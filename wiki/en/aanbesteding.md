# Procurement

## What does this mean?
This involves setting strict requirements for energy efficiency, product reusability, minimal environmental impact, and fair working conditions. Sustainable procurement also aims to minimize transport-related emissions by preferring local suppliers. Additionally, clear sustainability criteria are included in procurement contracts, and suppliers are regularly evaluated on their performance in this area.

- Selection of suppliers who offer products and services with low environmental impact and high social responsibility.
- Assessment criteria such as energy efficiency, reusability, and minimization of waste and CO₂ emissions.
- Mechanisms for evaluating suppliers' sustainability performance and improving their processes.
- Using local suppliers to limit transport emissions and including sustainable obligations in all contracts.

## How are we doing?
For all new procurements, we apply strict sustainability requirements to minimize our ecological and social impact. These requirements play a central role in our decision-making and are specifically tailored to IT solutions and hardware. The main sustainability requirements we include in new procurements are:

- **Energy efficiency**: We choose IT equipment and data center solutions that meet the highest standards for energy efficiency, such as devices with Energy Star certification or servers with low power consumption. This reduces both energy use and CO₂ emissions.
- **Lifespan and reusability**: We prefer hardware designed for longer lifespan and easy repair. This contributes to less replacement and reduces electronic waste. We also encourage the use of circular IT, where used equipment is redeployed or recycled.
- **Use of sustainable materials**: When purchasing IT equipment, we pay attention to the use of recycled and environmentally friendly materials, such as recycled plastic or metals that are responsibly sourced.
- **Software efficiency**: In addition to hardware, we also focus on software energy efficiency. This means we select solutions that make optimal use of system resources and minimize the load on data centers.
- **Packaging materials**: For new IT suppliers, we set requirements to minimize packaging materials, preferring fully recyclable or biodegradable packaging materials.
- **Transport and logistics**: We prefer local suppliers or suppliers that use sustainable logistics solutions, such as CO₂-neutral transport, to reduce the ecological footprint of delivery and installation.
- **Data center optimization**: For cloud and data center solutions, we choose suppliers that offer energy-efficient infrastructures, such as data centers with cooling through natural sources (such as water or air) and a focus on green energy sources.
