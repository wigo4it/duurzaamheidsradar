# CleanupFest

## What does this mean?
The CleanupFest is a bi-annual Wigo4it event where employees focus on cleaning up and optimizing cloud resources to save costs and energy consumption. The goal is always to achieve a minimum saving of €8000 per year (the cost of the event in hours), which amounts to a monthly saving of €667 or €22 per day. During the event, which lasts about four hours, teams work together to identify and remove unused or outdated resources, such as storage accounts, log analytics workspaces, environments, resource groups etc. The event also has a positive impact on employee awareness and behavior regarding cloud spending and sustainability. Benefits of the CleanupFest:

- Colleagues have a common positive goal and work in different team compositions.

- It increases awareness among employees about the importance of managing Cloud resources and the impact on costs and environment.

- Knowledge sharing about cloud management and best practices through active participation in the event.

## How are we doing?
We have executed the CleanupFest twice so far with stunning results. These results feel somewhat bittersweet, because if everyone had already had a sustainability mindset, we wouldn't have needed the CleanupFest. The summary after two CleanupFests:

- We will continue to do this 100% every six months.

- Total savings of more than 200,000 euros. Which amounts to 8% of our annual Cloud spend.

- We have thrown away too much sometimes :)

- So far only in Azure, next time also CleanupFest in Oracle Cloud.

- The CleanupFest receives many positive reactions on social media and even follow-up at other companies. This contributes to Wigo4it's name recognition and wanting to project authority.
