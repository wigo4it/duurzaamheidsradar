# Dual SIM Phone

## What does this mean?
A dual SIM phone is a device that offers the ability to use two different SIM cards, meaning users can combine both personal and business phone numbers in one phone. This provides convenience for people who would otherwise use two separate phones.

Benefits of Dual SIM:

- **Combining personal and business**: One phone can be used for both work and personal use, without having to carry two devices. You can turn off your work SIM and email when you don't want to be disturbed on your day off.
- **Reduced electronic waste**: Instead of two separate phones, only one device is needed, leading to less production and less waste from outdated devices.
- **Lower energy requirement**: By using only one phone instead of two, you consume less energy for charging and maintaining multiple devices.
