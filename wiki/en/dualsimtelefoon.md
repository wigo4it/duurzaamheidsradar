# Dual SIM Phone

## What does this mean?
A dual SIM phone is a device that allows the use of two different SIM cards, meaning users can combine both private and business phone numbers in one phone. This provides convenience for people who would otherwise carry two separate phones.

Advantages of Dual SIM:

- **Combine private and business**: One phone can be used for both work and personal life, without the need to carry two devices. You can switch off your work SIM and email when you don’t want to be disturbed on your day off.

- **Reduced electronic waste**: Instead of two separate phones, only one device is needed, which leads to less production and less waste from outdated devices.

- **Lower energy demand**: By using just one phone instead of two, you consume less energy for charging and maintaining multiple devices.
