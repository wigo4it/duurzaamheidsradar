# Deposit System

## What does this mean?
Since April 1, 2023, the Netherlands has introduced a deposit system for cans, in addition to the existing deposit on plastic bottles. This means consumers now pay 15 euro cents per can and small plastic bottle, and 25 euro cents for large plastic bottles.

Benefits of the deposit system:
- People are encouraged to return their empty cans and bottles instead of throwing them away. This helps reduce litter and keeps streets cleaner.
- Returned cans and bottles can be recycled and reused to make new packaging. This saves raw materials and energy, which is good for the environment.
- The deposit system increases consumer awareness about the importance of recycling and sustainability. People become more aware of their waste and its environmental impact.

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it has multiple refrigerators where our colleagues can take free soft drink cans. We have a separate collection bin for these cans. Once the bin is full, the cans are returned to a deposit machine at The Hague Central Station. The collected money is donated to the [Plastic Soup Foundation](https://www.plasticsoupfoundation.org/en/).

Benefits of our approach:
- **Environmental Impact**: We ensure 100% of our workplace cans are recycled
- **Social Responsibility**: Donations support marine conservation efforts
- **Team Engagement**: Colleagues actively participate in the sustainability initiative
