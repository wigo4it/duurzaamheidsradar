# Deposit system

## What does this mean?
Since April 1, 2023, the Netherlands has introduced a deposit system for cans, in addition to the existing deposit on plastic bottles. This means that consumers now pay €0.15 per can and small plastic bottle, and €0.25 for large plastic bottles.

Advantages of the deposit system:

People are encouraged to return their empty cans and bottles instead of throwing them away. This helps reduce litter and keeps the streets cleaner.

Returned cans and bottles can be recycled and reused to make new packaging. This saves raw materials and energy, which benefits the environment.

The deposit system raises consumer awareness about the importance of recycling and sustainability. People become more conscious of their waste and its impact on the environment.

## How are we doing?
Wigo4it has several refrigerators where our colleagues can take free cans of soda. We have a separate collection bin for these cans. Once the bin is full, the cans are returned to a Statiegeld Nederland machine at The Hague Central Station. The money collected is donated to the <a href="https://www.plasticsoupfoundation.org/doneer-statiegeld-aan-plastic-soup-foundation/#:~:text=Per%201%20juli%202021%20zit,milieuorganisaties%20jarenlang%20hard%20voor%20gemaakt.">Plastic Soup Foundation</a>.
We’ve been doing this for about a year and a half, and our takeaway so far is:

- The free drinks contribute to increased employee satisfaction.
- Donating feels good.
- Buying canned water is nonsense (we have tap water).
- We should look into healthier alternatives instead of soft drinks.
